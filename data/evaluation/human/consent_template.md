## 1. Purpose of the Study
You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating **IndicLearn**, an academic prototype designed to provide multilingual educational support (English, Telugu, Romanized Telugu, and Telugu-English code-mixed) for undergraduate computer science and engineering concepts.

The purpose of this study is to evaluate:
- The factual correctness, grounding, and clarity of system-generated explanations.
- The perceived relevance and transparency of adaptive practice questions and learning recommendations.
- The usability and navigational clarity of the interactive knowledge map dashboard.

This study does **not** evaluate your academic ability, intelligence, or university grades; it evaluates the performance and usability of the software system.

---

## 2. Participant Eligibility
To participate, you must:
1. Be currently enrolled in or recently graduated from an undergraduate engineering or computer science degree program (or closely related discipline).
2. Have academic familiarity with core computer science topics (e.g., Computer Networks, OSI Model, Layered Architectures).
3. Have working fluency in English and conversational or academic familiarity with Telugu (including Romanized script or code-mixed usage).

---

## 3. Study Procedures
If you choose to participate, you will be asked to:
1. Complete a brief anonymous background questionnaire (academic year, language familiarity).
2. Perform structured tasks using the IndicLearn interface (e.g., querying concepts in English/Telugu/Code-mixed, inspecting concept dependencies, taking an adaptive quiz, following a recommended learning action).
3. Complete an evaluation questionnaire assessing system clarity, navigational transparency, and language naturalness.
4. (For annotators only): Independently review generated answers against retrieved source documents and assign ratings across factual correctness, evidence support, and pedagogical clarity.

**Estimated Duration:** Approximately 30 to 45 minutes.

---

## 4. Risks and Benefits
- **Risks:** There are no known physical, legal, or psychological risks associated with this software evaluation beyond standard screen usage.
- **Benefits:** You will gain firsthand exposure to state-of-the-art multilingual educational NLP technology. Your feedback will directly inform research in low-resource and code-mixed educational technologies.

---

## 5. Privacy, Confidentiality, and Pseudonymization
- **Strict Anonymity:** We do **not** collect, store, or process any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, student ID number, Aadhaar number, phone number, email address, IP address, or residential address.
- **Pseudonymous Identifiers:** You will be assigned a random alphanumeric participant identifier (e.g., `PARTICIPANT-A8F2`) or annotator identifier (e.g., `ANN-01`). All interaction logs, task metrics, and survey responses will be linked exclusively to this pseudonymous identifier.
- **Data Sharing:** De-identified evaluation summaries, aggregate ratings, and inter-rater agreement statistics may be published in academic research papers and reproducible research repositories. No individual participant can be identified from published artifacts.

Participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate, skip any question or task, or discontinue your participation at any time without penalty or loss of benefits. Choosing to withdraw will have no effect on your academic standing, grades, or relationship with your institution.
